Design Head, Zomato

Vikram Desai

"Design Thinking for Startups"

About the Speaker

Vikram Desai is an iconic, award-winning designer fundamentally reshaping how consumers interact with food-tech apps. As the Design Head at Zomato, his minimalist and highly functional design philosophy has set industry standards for intuitive user experience.

With a background stretching across industrial, web, and sensory design, Vikram brings a uniquely holistic perspective to product building. He believes that great design is inherently invisible, removing friction and bringing pure delight to everyday interactions.

Session Details

Join Vikram as they delve deep into "Design Thinking for Startups". This session will cover the latest trends, invaluable insights from their journey, and actionable advice for students looking to excel in the UI/UX & Design sector.
